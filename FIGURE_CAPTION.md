# Figure caption

**Figure X. AI-related grant prevalence across Australian research funders, 2022–2025.** **a)** Proportion of all NHMRC and MRFF grants classified as AI-related (NHMRC: 105/2,866, 3.7%; MRFF: 57/1,121, 5.1%). **b)** Among ARC grants classified as AI-related, the proportion also classified as molecular or related life science (30/372, 8.1%). AI classification required either an AI-related Field of Research (FoR), explicit AI/machine-learning terminology, or a named AI/ML method in the grant record; broader terms such as *digital twin*, *predictive modelling*, *radiomics* and *automated image analysis* were used to flag records for review and were not counted automatically. Molecular/related life-science classification required either a biological/life-science FoR or predefined molecular/biological terminology in the grant text; general clinical or health research alone was insufficient. Counts aggregate grants assigned to 2022–2025 inclusive using funder-specific year fields (ARC funding commencement year, NHMRC source/application-round year, and MRFF contract start year).
